Workshops

A

Critical Thinking (Biblical and Theological Foundations)

A1

When Church and Faith Disappoint

Have you ever been hurt by the church or doubted what you believe? You’re not alone, and your hard questions are welcome here. Join us as we talk honestly about church disappointment and discover how to rebuild a honest, lasting faith.

A2

How to Think Theologically in Your Life

Think theology is just for pastors and thick books? Think again! Discover how asking "Where is God in this?" can transform your daily choices, campus life, and movie nights.

A3

Me Too/Church Too: Power Struggles in Churches

Abuse, silence, and power plays happen even inside churches—and staying quiet doesn't help. Join us to explore how we can pursue truth, justice, and real healing for those who have been hurt.

A4

How to Healthily Navigate Power Structures in the Church and Relationships with Church Leaders

How do you respect spiritual authority without losing your own voice? Learn how to set healthy boundaries, handle conflict wisely, and build constructive relationships with church leaders.

A5

The Three Worlds Approach to ScriptureThe World Before the Text, the World of the Text, and the World After the Text

Want to bring the Bible to life instead of just reading words on a page? Learn a simple 3-step lens to uncover the history behind the text, the story inside it, and what it means for your life today.

B

Growth (Calling and Adulthood)

B6

Discerning My Vocation

Struggling with "What job should I get?" Go deeper! Inspired by Joseph’s story, discover who God created you to be, how your gifts fit in, and whom you are called to live for.

B7

Why School and Work Are Important

"Why am I studying this?" If you’ve ever wondered if your major actually matters to God, this workshop is for you! Learn how your studies, late-night cramming, and future job connect to God’s bigger purpose for the world.

B8

Becoming an Adult in Uncertain Times

Career anxiety, money stress, moving out, and growing up—adulthood feels scary. Get a practical and biblical survival guide to help you transition into adulthood with confidence.

B9

AI, Work, and Human Dignity

Will AI replace your dream job? What actually makes us human when machines do so much? Explore how Christians can face rapid tech changes with hope, purpose, and human dignity.

C

Spiritual and Emotional Care

C10

How to Cope with Setbacks in Life

From broken friendships and harsh rejections to unmet dreams—life can hit hard. Come gain practical tools and biblical wisdom to navigate painful emotions without losing your faith or yourself.

C11

Loneliness, Belonging, and the Digital Self

Always online, yet feeling completely alone? You're not the only one. Unpack why we feel so lonely in a hyper-connected world and how real community offers a place to truly belong.

C12

Healthy Relationships and Communication in the University

Navigating campus friendships, dating, and setting boundaries can be tricky. Build a solid biblical framework to communicate clearly, resolve conflicts, and grow healthy relationships.

C13

Holistic Family Connections

Dealing with helicopter parents or constant family tension? Learn how to honor your parents while setting healthy boundaries and breaking free from toxic dynamics as an independent adult.

C14

AI as a Counselling Tool

Turning to ChatGPT for emotional support late at night? Explore the rise of AI mental health tools, where they help, where they fall short, and how they challenge human spiritual care.

D

Engaging Society and Culture

D15

Witnessing Christ in the UniversityEngaging the University

How can you live out your faith naturally on campus without sounding preachy? Get equipped with practical tools and courage to represent Christ and share hope in your university context.

D16

Power, Politics, and the Christian Witness

Wars, elections, and national tensions—how should followers of Jesus navigate political chaos? Learn how to care about justice and peace while keeping our ultimate loyalty to God’s kingdom.

D17

Individualism, Secularism, and the Self

Caught between Asian family expectations ("do what's best for the family") and Western self-expression ("do whatever makes you happy")? Discover how the Gospel offers a better way to find yourself.

D18

International Students and Belonging Across Borders

Are you studying abroad as an international student, or a local student looking to make global friends on campus? Come connect! Together, we’ll discover how international students’ unique "exile" journeys and local students' radical hospitality can create a vibrant, cross-cultural community where everyone truly belongs.

D19

Social Media and Gospel Witness

Can your Instagram, TikTok, or social media posts actually point people to Jesus? Learn creative ways to use digital spaces to share hope, practice authenticity, and be a light online.

D20

Responding to Different Faiths in a Diverse World

Surrounded by friends with totally different religions or worldviews? Learn how to share your convictions with courage and humility, moving beyond passive "tolerance" to genuine conversation.

D21

Gender Identity Across the World

Gender issues are viewed differently across East Asia and the world. Explore how Christians in different contexts face these conversations with biblical truth, cultural sensitivity, and grace.

D22

Power and Creation Care

Environmental crises aren't just science problems—they are justice and power issues. Discover how caring for God’s creation challenges greed and systemic exploitation in today’s world.

D23

Trauma Issues: Personal and Social

Deep personal heartbreak and collective societal trauma run deep. Learn how faith communities can become safe places for emotional honesty, healing, and restoring justice.
